# Solar Shine SA

Build a complete premium, professional USA-style website for Solar Vibes SA, a Texas-based solar panel cleaning & maintenance company.

TECH

React + Vite

Tailwind CSS

Lucide React icons

Responsive on desktop, tablet & mobile

Clean reusable components

Keep the project lightweight; no unnecessary libraries

BUSINESS

Solar Vibes SA
Professional services:

Solar Panel Cleaning

Solar Panel Maintenance

Animal/Bird Protection

Leak Inspections

Texas
Phone: 210-891-9457

DESIGN

Make it look human-made, premium, modern and trustworthy, NOT like a generic AI/template website.

Use:

White/off-white background

Charcoal text

Subtle solar yellow/gold accents

High-quality realistic solar/Texas photography

Strong typography

Clean spacing

Elegant subtle animations

Premium American home-service aesthetic

Avoid excessive gradients, excessive rounded cards, fake statistics, fake reviews, fake certifications or fake company claims.

SECTIONS

1. Navbar
Logo/name + Home, Services, Our Work, About, Videos, FAQ, Contact + prominent Call 210-891-9457 button. Responsive mobile menu.

2. Hero
Headline:
Keep Your Solar Panels Working at Their Best.

Text:
Professional solar panel cleaning, maintenance, animal protection and leak inspections across Texas.

Buttons:
Get a Free Quote
Call 210-891-9457

3. Services
Create premium sections for all 4 services with relevant imagery and concise professional descriptions.

4. Before & After — IMPORTANT
Create an interactive Before/After solar panel comparison slider with draggable mouse/touch divider. Make it easy to replace the images later. Add CTA: Request a Cleaning.

5. Why Solar Care
Explain how Texas dust, dirt, pollen, bird/animal debris and weather exposure can affect solar panels. Use an editorial image-based layout, not a generic card grid.

6. About Us
Create a professional About section explaining that Solar Vibes SA helps Texas homeowners/businesses maintain and protect their solar investment through cleaning, maintenance, animal protection and leak inspections. Do not invent years of experience or certifications.

7. Process
Simple 3 steps:
Schedule → Inspect → Get It Done

8. Facebook Videos — IMPORTANT
Add both provided Facebook Reel embeds in a premium responsive section titled See Solar Vibes SA in Action.

VIDEO 1:

VIDEO 2:

Desktop: side-by-side. Mobile: stacked.

9. Texas Service Area
Premium Texas-focused section saying Solar Vibes SA serves customers across Texas. Do not invent specific cities.

10. FAQ
Accordion with questions about cleaning frequency, cleaning benefits, animal protection, leak inspections, residential/commercial service and requesting a quote.

11. Final CTA
Ready to Give Your Solar Panels a Fresh Start?
Buttons: Get a Free Quote / Call 210-891-9457

12. Contact
Professional quote form:
Name, Phone, Email, Service Needed, Message.
Add validation and clear success/error states. Phone CTA must use tel:+12108919457.

13. Footer
Solar Vibes SA, services summary, navigation, phone and © 2026.

FINAL REQUIREMENTS

Complete everything in one implementation.

No Lorem Ipsum or unfinished sections.

All buttons/navigation must work.

Before/after slider must work on touch + mouse.

Facebook embeds must be responsive.

Excellent mobile design with no horizontal overflow.

Add SEO title/meta description.

Use realistic imagery and make image areas easy to replace with real client photos.

Keep animations subtle and professional.

Do not add unnecessary features or dependencies.

Do not stop until the complete website is implemented.
